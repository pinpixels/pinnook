Pinnook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Applies to Pinnook version 1.0.0 and later

Pinnook is a browser extension that lets you keep private notes, tags and projects on top of web pages. It is built to work entirely on your own device. This policy explains, in plain language, what that means.

The short version

  • We do not collect your data. Pinnook has no servers, no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no crash reporting.
  • Your notes stay on your device, inside your browser profile, unless you turn on the optional Premium encrypted backup.
  • We never sell, rent, share or use your data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or any purpose unrelated to the single purpose of the extension: keeping notes about web pages you choose to save.
  • The free version cannot send data anywhere. It contains no code that makes network requests, and the extension's Content Security Policy blocks outside connections.

Who is responsible

Pinnook is published by PinPixels ("we", "us"). Contact: pinpixels@gmail.com · GitHub issues.

Because we do not receive your notes or browsing information, in almost all cases you are the only controller of the information you keep in Pinnook.

Pinnook does not record pages you do not save, does not read your browser history, does not read the content of web pages, and does not run inside Incognito windows.

To show the right note for the tab you are looking at, Pinnook uses the browser's tabs permission to read the address and title of your tabs. That information is processed on your device only, to look up your own notes and to show the “this page has a note” badge. It is not stored unless you save the page, and it is never transmitted.

When you select text and choose Add selection to this page's Pinnook note, the selected text is added to that note on your device.

What leaves your device

Free version: nothing.

Premium version (optional): only if you activate a licence and connect a storage provider yourself.

  1. Encrypted backup. Pinnook encrypts your notes, tags, projects and page addresses on your device (AES-256-GCM, key derived from your passphrase with PBKDF2-SHA256, 600 000 iterations, random salt and IV) and uploads the encrypted file pinnook-backup.enc to your own Google Drive or Dropbox account. The provider, and we, cannot read it. Your passphrase is never sent anywhere and is not stored on disk; if you tick “remember”, it is kept in memory only until you close the browser. If you lose your passphrase, the backup cannot be recovered.
  2. Provider sign-in. Sign-in happens on the provider's own page. Pinnook receives an access token (and, for Dropbox, a refresh token) which is stored on your device and sent only to that provider's API. Disconnecting revokes the token.
  3. Licence check. Your licence key is verified on your device using a public key built into the extension. No server is contacted.

We have no access to the backup file or to your provider account.

Google user data (Limited Use disclosure)

If you connect Google Drive, Pinnook requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, which lets it see and modify only the single backup file that Pinnook itself creates. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Pinnook's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup feature you asked for; it is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by people.

Dropbox

If you connect Dropbox, Pinnook uses a private “App folder” and can access only the files inside it. Pinnook is an independent product and is not affiliated with Dropbox, Inc.

Purchases (Premium)

Premium is sold through a third-party payment provider that acts as the seller of record (named at checkout: a merchant of record that will be named here before Premium goes on sale). The provider collects your payment details, email address and tax information under its own privacy policy. We never see your card number. We may receive your order reference and email address from the provider to deliver your licence key and provide support.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

None. The extension sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, fonts or images, and embeds no analytics. Our documentation website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical visitor data under GitHub's privacy statement.

How we use information

We do not receive your Pinnook data, so we do not use it. Information you send us voluntarily (for example a support email or a public GitHub issue) is used only to answer you and improve Pinnook. Please do not post private notes or personal data in public issues.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with anyone, because we do not have it. We do not transfer or use user data for any purpose other than providing and improving the single purpose of the extension, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Retention and deletion

You are in control:

  • Remove a page in the panel to delete its note and tags.
  • Settings → Your data → Delete all Pinnook data wipes everything from this browser.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its local data from the browser.
  • Premium backup: disconnect in Settings, then delete pinnook-backup.enc from your Google Drive or Dropbox app folder.

Security

  • Content Security Policy forbids inline scripts, remote code and (in the free version) all outbound connections.
  • Notes are rendered with a minimal Markdown renderer that escapes all HTML; scripts, images and raw HTML cannot run from a note.
  • Premium encryption uses the browser's built-in, audited WebCrypto implementation; no third-party cryptography code is included.
  • You can check what the extension is allowed to do yourself: its permissions are listed on its Chrome Web Store page and on chrome://extensions → Pinnook → Details.

No system is perfectly secure. Anyone with access to your unlocked computer and browser profile can read your local notes, as with any local data; consider your operating-system account and disk encryption.

Children

Pinnook is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal data. Pinnook keeps your data on your own device, so you can exercise these rights yourself at any time with the in-app Export and Delete tools. For data held by our payment provider, contact them or us and we will help you reach them. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above, describe the change in the changelog, and, if a change affects how your data is handled, highlight it in the extension's store listing and release notes before it takes effect. The full history is public in the project's Git repository.
